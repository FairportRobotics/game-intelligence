# 578 Game Intelligence

This context brings FRC competition results and team observations together to help Fairport Robotics prepare for matches.

## Competition

**Team**:
An FRC team identified by its official team number.

**Season**:
An annual FRC competition cycle organized around a particular game.

**Event**:
A specific FRC competition at which teams play scheduled matches.

**Match**:
An official contest between Red and Blue alliances at an event.

**Alliance**:
A group of teams assigned to one side of a match.

**Autonomous**:
The opening match period in which robot actions run without driver control.

**End Game**:
The closing portion of match play, with game-specific scoring activities.

**Official result**:
A match result published by FIRST.

**Teleop**:
The driver-controlled match period following Autonomous.

## Ratings and outcomes

**TrueSkill rating**:
An estimate of a team's competitive skill that includes uncertainty about that estimate.

**Phase rating**:
A team's competitive-skill estimate for one match phase, inferred from the results of alliances it joined in that phase.

**All-time rating**:
A team's rating based on its match history across supported seasons.

**Season rating**:
A team's rating based only on matches from one FRC season.

**Elo rating**:
A team rating updated from match outcomes using the Elo rating system.

**EPA**:
Statbotics' Expected Points Added estimate of a team's scoring contribution, expressed in points.
_Avoid_: OPC, Statbotics Elo

**Match outcome probability**:
The estimated probability that Red wins, the match ties, or Blue wins.

## Scouting

**Match scouting record**:
A scout's structured observation of a team's performance in a particular match.

**Pit scouting record**:
A scout's observation of a team's robot or capabilities at a particular event.

**Pre-match note**:
Information entered after reviewing recorded matches to prepare for competition, either as reusable team context or for a specific upcoming match.
